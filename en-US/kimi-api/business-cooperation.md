# Business cooperation & sales

Kimi Open Platform offers a range of partnership options for enterprise clients to meet business needs at any scale.

## Are dedicated products and services available for business customers?

### Kimi API enterprise services

The Kimi sales team can provide additional resources and support for business customers using the Kimi API. Please fill out the form at [https://platform.kimi.ai/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.ai/contact-sales) to contact sales.

### Kimi Business

Kimi Assistant now offers Kimi Business membership benefits. Visit [https://www.kimi.ai/membership/pricing](https://www.kimi.ai/membership/pricing) to subscribe online.

## How to contact the sales team?

Visit [platform.kimi.ai/contact-sales](https://platform.kimi.ai/contact-sales) to submit a contact form. Our sales team will reach out within 1–2 business days.

<Callout type="tip">
**Include in your submission**:
- Company name and industry
- Estimated usage volume
- Primary use cases
- Preferred partnership model
</Callout>

## Contract signing

Organization-verified users can enter into a formal service agreement with Moonshot:
- Both standard service agreements and custom contracts are available.
- After signing, you gain access to wire transfer payments and enterprise-grade SLAs.
- Contact the sales team to discuss the specific process.

## Volume discounts

High-volume enterprise clients can apply for exclusive discounts:
| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Eligibility** | Organization verification + signed contract |
| **Discount levels** | Based on committed usage volume and contract duration |
| **Application** | Apply through sales team with estimated monthly usage |

## Enterprise API service

Enterprise API service builds on the standard API offering with:
- **Higher rate limits and concurrency quotas**
- **Dedicated technical support channel**
- **Custom SLA guarantees**
- **Early access to new features**
